# Focusa Commercial Licensing

Focusa is source-available under `LICENSE.md` for personal, educational, evaluation, and non-commercial local use.

A paid commercial license is required for:

- company or team use;
- use in paid client delivery or consulting engagements;
- production use;
- hosted service or managed agent platform use;
- embedding Focusa in a commercial product;
- redistribution or resale;
- internal use where Focusa materially supports commercial operations.

Commercial licenses may include support, private deployment terms, team/enterprise rights, and custom adapter rights. See `SUPPORT_TERMS.md` for support expectations and `legal/COMMERCIAL_LICENSE_TEMPLATE.md` for the draft agreement structure.

To request commercial licensing, use `https://install.focusa.dev/license` or contact `support@focusa.dev`.
