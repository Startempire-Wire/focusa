# Focusa Support Terms

These support terms describe the intended commercial support model. Specific commitments require a signed order form or commercial license.

## Community / Evaluation

For source-available personal, educational, and evaluation use:

- no guaranteed response time;
- no production support;
- bug reports may be reviewed at Startempire Wire's discretion;
- external patches require the approved contributor process in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Commercial Preview

Typical commercial preview support may include:

- private setup guidance;
- installation/bootstrap assistance;
- Workpoint Method onboarding;
- issue triage for licensed use;
- prioritized fixes for license-blocking defects.

## Team / Enterprise

Team or enterprise support may include:

- private deployment support;
- custom harness adapters;
- audit/export requirements;
- retention or compliance workflows;
- agreed response times;
- private support channel.

## Exclusions

Unless explicitly agreed, support does not include:

- legal advice;
- guaranteed uptime for self-hosted deployments;
- support for modified forks;
- emergency production support for unlicensed usage;
- third-party model, harness, hosting, or infrastructure failures.
