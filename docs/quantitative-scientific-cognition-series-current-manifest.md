## 2. Primitive ownership laws

1. **Spec 137 owns time.** No mathematical, statistical, physical, prediction, or vertical subsystem creates a second clock authority.
2. **Spec 137B owns high-resolution temporal truth.** Displayed precision, UUID order, database insertion order, or provider timestamps cannot substitute for measured accuracy and causal lineage.
3. **Spec 152 owns mathematical representation.** Verticals may register specialized models but cannot redefine quantities, units, dimensions, exactness, solver proof, or numerical validity.
4. **Spec 152A owns statistical inference.** Prediction, research, markets, software, finance, and physics consume one probability, sampling, uncertainty, and causal-analysis substrate.
5. **Spec 153 owns physical-model semantics.** Robotics, energy, structures, fluids, circuits, sailing, and other physical verticals extend shared physical primitives rather than forking them.
6. **Spec 138 owns forecast commitment and learning.** Mathematical or physical calculations do not become predictions until committed through Spec 138 authority.
7. **Spec 138B owns prediction-specific adversarial assurance.** Generic champion/challenger infrastructure may be supplied by other specs, but blind forecast sealing, matched information sets, evidence-response evaluation, and forecast promotion are defined here.
8. **Spec 140 owns runtime identity.** Every consequential computation, simulation, prediction, experiment, and verification binds the exact active constitution, instruction graph, model/tool identity, and approved target profile.
9. **Spec 144 owns formal-semantic and verification truth.** It compiles quantitative, statistical, physical, temporal, and prediction requirements into complete independent verification portfolios.
10. **The reducer owns canonical state.** Secondary Cognition, Intuition, tools, clients, and external engines emit proposals, results, findings, and Evidence; they do not bypass reducer admission or settlement.
11. **Verticals own domain interpretation.** Market, finance, engineering, research, real estate, legal-operations, robotics, sailing, energy, and other packs define domain variables, policies, constants, authorities, and specialized models without duplicating core primitives.

## 5. Core constitutional laws

1. A number without semantic meaning is not a quantitative fact.
2. A measured quantity requires unit, dimension, source, uncertainty, and time.
3. Displayed digits do not establish accuracy.
4. Exact, approximate, estimated, simulated, inferred, predicted, and observed values remain distinct.
5. Every equation and model has an applicability and validity envelope.
6. Every solver has tolerances, convergence criteria, resource bounds, and failure modes.
7. Dimensional inconsistency blocks before calculation.
8. Probability and uncertainty are not interchangeable with model confidence.
9. Simulation is not observation.
10. Statistical significance is not practical significance or causal proof.
11. Correlation is not a physical mechanism.
12. A physical analogy cannot masquerade as a physical law.
13. Unknown quantities and parameters remain unknown.
14. Ill-posed, unidentified, or underdetermined problems must be refused, bounded, or reformulated.
15. Conjectures remain conjectures until independently tested.
16. A counterexample can invalidate an overbroad rule even when average performance is favorable.
17. High-consequence deterministic loops cannot contain an LLM or remote conversational dependency.
18. Learning cannot be promoted from temporally contaminated, statistically invalid, numerically unstable, physically impossible, or semantically incomplete evidence.
19. Human-facing mathematical explanation must be inspectable without exposing private chain-of-thought.
20. Every canonical state change is reducer-visible, replayable, Evidence-linked, and Receipt-backed.

## 11. Acceptance and closure

This series cannot claim full implementation or closure while any of the following is true:

- the original 138B adversarial forecasting requirements are absent or weakened;
- a quantitative result lacks typed quantity, units, dimensions, provenance, uncertainty, or temporal grounding where applicable;
- a statistics operation lacks a frozen protocol or valid applicability decision;
- a physical claim lacks model validity, measurement authority, or required experimental support;
- high-resolution market claims rely on displayed precision rather than calibrated accuracy;
- a legacy prediction route can bypass strict authority in a high-consequence profile;
- ontology modules exist only as hard-coded client vocabularies;
- reflexes exist only as registry names without executable providers;
- Secondary Cognition can mutate canonical state;
- reducer events, persistence, replay, Evidence, or Receipts are missing;
- Pi, API, CLI, MCP, generated clients, or Mission Canvas omit activated capabilities;
- Spec 144 leaves a mandatory quantitative, statistical, physical, temporal, or prediction obligation uncovered;
- simulation, inference, prediction, and observation are flattened into one claim class;
- conjectures or correlations are promoted as laws or mechanisms;
- human learning is claimed from decorative equations without concept, derivation, practice, and transfer evidence.

Only a complete, verified, cross-surface, replayable implementation may claim conformance.
