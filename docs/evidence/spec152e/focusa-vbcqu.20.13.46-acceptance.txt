Bounded result
- Added the shared account/product contract (`uiai_activation.rs`): exactly
  one verified EDD account identity (`UiaiAccountIdentity` with account_id +
  edd_customer_id) is used for UIAI activation — no duplicate customer
  identity is ever created. The mapping is server-owned: the client submits
  only the public product code `uiai_operator_lifetime_v1`; EDD ids, prices,
  tiers, grants, limits, and commercial flags are never accepted
  (`resolve_uiai_activation` has no such inputs). A Focusa-only account
  (exactly one product, `focusa`) gets `UiaiGrantRequired` — Focusa-only can
  never activate UIAI. An inactive/unbound UIAI grant gets
  `UiaiGrantInvalid`, a wrong requested code gets `ProductMappingRequired`,
  and any requested feature/limit outside the exact subset of the independent
  `uiai-engine` grant gets `ProductIsolationViolation` (the Focusa grant
  never satisfies UIAI scope).
- Added the same-account UIAI adapter posture: `from_same_edd_account_uiai_authority`
  enforces `same_account_binding` (both the Focusa parent lease and the UIAI
  grant lease subject ids equal the single account id), exact grants from the
  UIAI projection, and the same-registration child-token receipt settlement.
- Extended the child-token broker: `validate_request` now fails closed with
  `AccountMismatch` when lease subject evidence shows two different accounts
  (or one side proves an account and the other does not), and the strict
  `validate_same_account_binding` guard requires both leases to carry the one
  verified EDD account identity.
- Plumbed the lease subject onto `EntitlementSnapshot` (`subject_id` from the
  signed lease payload), so same-account routing and product isolation are
  decided from authority truth, never from local/self-issued entitlement.
- Added 8 Rust unit tests (uiai_activation 5, uiai_child_token 3) proving the
  Focusa-only denial, exact-grant activation without duplicate customer
  identity, wrong-code / isolation / invalid-grant denials, strict
  same-account binding, and the frozen vector grid staying green.
- No unverified-email promotion, no local/self-issued entitlement, no
  client-controlled EDD price/grants, no independent facade authority, no
  raw or unmasked real-email evidence anywhere. Cargo/release builds remain
  deferred until the operator's 50% gate.
