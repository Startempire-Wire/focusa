  facade plugin/routes contract. Nine named install-site surfaces (activation
  start, verify, offers, checkout, existing license, poll, refresh, nodes, and
  account links) map to eleven authority proxy actions: activation_start,
  activation_verify, activation_offers, activation_select_offer,
  activation_checkout, activation_existing_license, activation_poll,
  lease_refresh, nodes_list, nodes_deactivate, and account_manage_link.
- Every install-site page+method resolves to the registered focusa_install_v1
  facade path (registry paths for verification/checkout/manage and the six
  success/cancel/recovery render pages) and to an exact /v1/ authority proxy
  route from the facade registry. There is no local issuance route; every
  operation derives from a WPUIAI.com EDD authority response.
- Proxy requests are server-owned: facade_id, origin, and product_code come
  from the registry binding, never from the caller; operation inputs are the
  exact call-stack allowlists; identity, commerce, product, price, grant,
  limit, credential, and redirect fields are rejected (FACADE_REQUEST_FIELD_DENIED).
- Responses are masked before render: only bounded public fields pass through;
  raw email, license keys, credentials, card data, and internal EDD records
  are never forwarded. When the upstream authority is unavailable the facade
  fails closed with a bounded health/recovery envelope (AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE,
  retry, recovery-only posture, safe recovery link) and never issues a local
  license, node, or lease.
