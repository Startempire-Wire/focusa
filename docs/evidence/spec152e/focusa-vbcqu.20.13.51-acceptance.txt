Bounded result
- Added the legacy-customer migration workflow: openChallenge() invites
  (canonical / evidence_backed_import / verify_first) or challenges (duplicate /
  synthetic_quarantine / refunded_revoked / unresolved) a legacy EDD/install
  record by opaque inventory handle, storing only the keyed email lookup digest
  and a pinned evidence digest — never the raw email. One challenge per record
  handle; repeated opens replay or dedupe and never create a second challenge.
- attachVerified() attaches a record ONLY after mailbox verification and
  evidence-backed resolution: the verified registration email must equal the
  challenge's keyed digest, and the EDD customer merge is delegated to the
  promotion service's mergeLegacyVerified (verified identity -> authority
  account -> existing EDD customer -> purchase links). Purchase/license history
  is preserved: EDD customer/order/item/license rows are byte-identical before
  and after migration, and order/license references are journaled on the
  attachment. Verified legacy customers merge once: replays and repeated
  canonical requests return the stored attachment (replayed=true / existing=true)
  with exactly one attachment row.
- Conflicts quarantine without entitlement loss: ACCOUNT_MERGE_REVIEW_REQUIRED,
  EDD_ORDER_UNVERIFIED, EDD_LICENSE_UNVERIFIED, EDD_LICENSE_UNUSABLE, and
  LICENSE_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH failures journal the record quarantined with the
  fail-closed reason and write zero account/identity/customer/entitlement state;
  EDD truth stays untouched. quarantine-only dispositions (synthetic_quarantine,
  refunded_revoked, unresolved) can never attach even with a verified email.
  quarantineRecord() quarantines unverified records explicitly; reopenQuarantined()
  keeps every quarantined record recoverable, and a reopened record must again
  pass verification, evidence, and the disposition gate. Quarantined or
  never-attached records cannot activate new nodes (legacy activation adapter
  still demands mailbox verification plus a usable owner license; revoked,
  refunded, synthetic, and unverified records all fail closed).
- The migration journal (wpuiai_legacy_customer_challenges / _attachments /
  _journal) is append-audited and preservation-only on rollback. Results,
  journals, and the fixture contain no raw email, no license key, no secret
  prefix, and no synthetic focusa_live key material. No unverified-email
  promotion, no local/self-issued entitlement, no independent facade authority,
  and no client-controlled EDD price/grant exists in this surface; the exact
  existing typed error codes are reused (no new error codes were registered).
