Bounded result
- Added FocusaSpec152eEddCustomerAdapter: resolves or creates EDD customers only from
  verified identity (mailbox_verified or account_promoted). Works over wp_edd_customers
  and wp_edd_customer_email_addresses, plus optional WordPress user IDs and Stripe
  customer references. Returns typed account/customer references with resolution
  disposition (existing/new). Never enumerates accounts; lookup is exact-email only.
  Idempotent via wp_wpuiai_edd_customer_idempotency with digest conflict detection.
- Unverified identities (email_verification_pending) are rejected with
  EMAIL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED. Missing or empty migration provenance is rejected.
  Invalid email formats are rejected. Idempotency key replay with a changed payload
  (different email) is denied with IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT.
- New verified users create exactly one EDD customer with provenance notes and
  secondary email address record. Existing customers by primary email or secondary
  email address are resolved deterministically without creating duplicates.
- Customer count is bounded for verification only; no enumeration method is exposed.
