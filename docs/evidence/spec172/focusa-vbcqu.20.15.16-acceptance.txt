Bounded result
- Lifecycle transition matrix (Spec 172 section 21) is the single authority:
  `refund` -> refunded (terminal, adverse, sequence +1, 30-day window, whole-order
  only), `chargeback` -> refunded (terminal, adverse, +1, no customer window),
  `revoke` -> revoked (terminal, adverse, +1, no customer window), `complete` and
  `unsuspend` are never adverse and can never run against a terminal Bundle.
- 30-day whole-order Bundle refund only: the canonical EDD refund truth adapter
  derives `scope=whole_order` exclusively from canonical rows (order status refunded
  + exactly one complete whole-order refund row whose amount equals the order total);
  a component-level (item-scoped) refund fails closed with COMPONENT_REFUND_UNSUPPORTED,
  a refund outside the 30-day window fails closed with REFUND_WINDOW_EXPIRED, and
  absent/partial/unmapped refund truth fails closed with REFUND_TRUTH_UNKNOWN — all
  journaled `denied` with zero sequence bump and paid grants intact.
- Each adverse event settles once; both Bundle grants revoke together: a whole-order
  refund, a lost Stripe dispute (chargeback), and a manual revoke each produce
  `decision=applied`, `to_state=refunded|revoked`, `grants_revoked=2`, and
  `paid_grants_active=false` — the exact two underlying Operator v1 grants
  (focusa_operator_lifetime_v1 + uiai_operator_lifetime_v1) are removed together.
  The authority sequence advances by exactly one per applied settlement (projection
  sequence 1 -> settlement sequence 2, verified per account). Duplicate redeliveries
  (same event, new idempotency key), idempotency-key replays, and a second adverse
  event on an already-terminal Bundle (e.g. revoke after refund) are journaled
  `replayed` with zero bump and applied count stays exactly one per event.
- Stale entitlement can never reactivate: after a terminal settlement, a stale
  `complete`/`unsuspend` cache event fails closed with
  LICENSE_TERMINAL_REACTIVATION_DENIED (zero state change, zero bump), a genuinely
  new event whose authority ordinal is not newer than the account's highest sequence
  fails closed with ENTITLEMENT_SEQUENCE_ROLLBACK_DENIED, and the assertion
  transition fixture rejects a stale paid credential captured before the settlement
  (PAID_GRANT_REVOKED when terminal / STALE_CREDENTIAL_SUPERSEDED on higher
  sequence). Chargeback/revoke therefore propagate through stale cache.
- Customer data and recovery remain accessible: all 9 customers, 9 orders, 9 order
  items, 9 canonical licenses, 6 refund rows, 9 Bundle projections, 9 authority
  accounts, and 9 verified registrations are preserved (zero deletions); every
  account stays `active`; registrations stay `mailbox_verified` at
  `entitlement_issued`; and the limited posture keeps the permanent safety
  allowances (read_projection, basic_customer_data_export, account_control,
  device_control, license_status, diagnostics, repair, rollback,
  stable_security_update, uninstall) so recovery/export/repair/rollback/security/
  uninstall remain available. Still-verified accounts return to
  `verified_no_license`; unverified accounts return to `unverified` (registration
  only, never a grant).
- Paid -> limited assertion transition fixture: the paid credential derives
  exclusively from the ACTIVE composite projection (both grants, one human key,
  whole-order 30-day refund policy, bounded 90-day refresh window) and can never be
  derived after settlement; the limited assertion carries ONLY the frozen limited
  families (6 Focusa manual_* + 6 UIAI public_* from
  spec172-verified-limited-access.v1.yaml) plus the 10 permanent allowances, verifies
  with the server-owned Ed25519 key, and rejects validly-signed widening
  (LIMITED_FAMILY_WIDENING_DENIED) and tampering (LIMITED_SIGNATURE_INVALID).
- Idempotent outbox and reconciliation proof: every applied settlement appends one
  signed HMAC-SHA256 envelope in the same transaction as the sequence advance and
  settlement row; dispatch is exactly-once through the UNIQUE delivery ledger (4
  envelopes delivered, a tampered payload dead-letters on digest verification and is
  never delivered, second dispatch of the same envelope is impossible). The reconciler
  detects canonical refunded/revoked/disputed Bundle orders missing a settlement,
  repairs them evidence-safe from canonical EDD truth (dry run applies nothing; apply
  repairs exactly one missing settlement; a second apply run repairs zero —
  converged=true), and stably quarantines component/out-of-window/unknown-truth
  orders with their exact error code.
- No anonymous product capability, no local/self-issued grant, no caller-controlled
  product, price, refund scope/amount/date, License Type, grant, family, feature,
  limit, node, or commercial right (client commerce fields, raw email, and idempotency
  conflicts all fail closed), and no presenter-owned policy. No raw email, key,
  token, customer row, credential, or card data in journals, decisions, outbox,
  fixtures, or evidence (verified by regex scans).
