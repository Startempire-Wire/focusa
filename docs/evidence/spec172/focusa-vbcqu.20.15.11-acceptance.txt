Bounded result
- Issue only after verified account and node binding: `issue()` requires an
  active verified_no_license posture; the authority signs a real Ed25519
  (RFC 8032) assertion over the canonical payload binding posture, account,
  identity, product scope, node, family allowlist, monotonic sequence,
  issue/refresh times, and server-owned signer. Sequence and allowlist are
  server-owned; caller-supplied family input (`release_proof`) is ignored and
  the posture allowlist is signed instead. Missing posture fails with
  `EMAIL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`; post-revoke issuance fails with
  `VERIFIED_LIMITED_ACCESS`.
- Refresh rotates the bounded credential to the next monotonic sequence with a
  fresh refresh window WITHOUT imposing any access expiry on the permanent
  posture (status stays `issued`, expiry `none`) and WITHOUT widening the
  family allowlist; replay returns the same credential.
- Revoke (lost device / account abuse) is preservation-only: current assertion
  and posture flip status with an explicit reason; every assertion and posture
  row is preserved. Post-revoke verify fails with `VERIFIED_LIMITED_ACCESS`.
- Recover from verified identity: the account must already hold a verified
  posture for the product scope, that posture must be revoked/superseded, the
  recovery proof must be a fresh canonical `verified_at` after the account's
  latest posture change, and the node must be a fresh registration (max three
  per account). The recovered posture starts at a strictly higher monotonic
  account sequence with the same canonical limited-mode allowlist — recovery
  never widens into paid families, never widens into a product scope the
  account never held (`PRODUCT_NOT_INCLUDED`), never reactivates a registered
  node (`NODE_LIMIT_REACHED`), and never creates an EDD key.
- Verify fails closed on every acceptance vector: unverified
  (`EMAIL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` / `ASSERTION_UNKNOWN` server-side), tampered
  signature or payload (`SIGNATURE_INVALID`), stale pre-refresh sequence
  (`ENTITLEMENT_SEQUENCE_ROLLBACK_DENIED`), wrong node (`NODE_LIMIT_REACHED`),
  unknown family and paid family (`CAPABILITY_FAMILY_NOT_INCLUDED`), wrong
  product scope (`ENTITLEMENT_PRODUCT_MISMATCH`), wrong account binding
  (`ASSERTION_TAMPERED`), revoked credential (`VERIFIED_LIMITED_ACCESS`), and
  elapsed bounded refresh window (`CREDENTIAL_REFRESH_REQUIRED`). A validly
  re-signed wrong-node/wrong-family/wrong-product claim still fails closed
  (valid signatures do not grant policy).
- The Focusa client/store evaluates presented claims against authoritative
  posture state and persists ONLY verified assertions in a local mirror
  (deduplicated by posture+sequence); revoked, unverified, self-issued, and
  tampered claims are never persisted. It has no issue/sign capability: no
  local/self-issued grant is possible.
- Cross-language fixture: the PHP candidate regenerates the committed fixture
  byte-identically (deterministic Ed25519 with a fixed synthetic seed), and
  the Python vector test independently derives the same Ed25519 public key and
  verifies all 13 valid signatures with Python cryptography before applying the
  same fail-closed policy evaluation. Both implementations agree on every
  verdict (4 valid, 11 denied).
- No EDD Software Licensing key and no zero-dollar fake license is created:
  `wp_edd_licenses` stays at zero and `assertEddFree()` passes by schema
  introspection. No raw email, secret key, customer row, or credential data
  appears in any returned envelope or the fixture.
