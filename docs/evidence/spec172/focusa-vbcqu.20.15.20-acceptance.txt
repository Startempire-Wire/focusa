Bounded result
- Added `OperatorFamilyInheritanceDecision` enum with seven stable variants:
  Inherit, ExcludedPendingAssignment, DeniedUnknownProduct, DeniedUnknownOwner,
  DeniedUnknownSideEffect, DeniedFutureProduct, DeniedMateriallyNewHostedCost.
  Each variant carries a stable `label()` and participates in `is_inherited()`
  and `is_denied()` classification.
- Added `classify_operator_family_inheritance(product, family, known_product,
  known_operator_family, known_owner, known_side_effect, hosted_cost)`:
  deterministic Spec 172 Section 8.2/8.3 classifier. The five conditions of
  8.2 must ALL be true for inheritance; otherwise the classifier fails closed
  with the appropriate denial variant. Callers supply factual classification
  inputs; they NEVER supply product, price, family, feature, limit, node, or
  commercial right.
- Added `SPEC172_FOCUSA_OPERATOR_V1_FAMILIES`: the 10 canonical Focusa Operator
  v1 families (6 manual + 4 premium). New operations in these families inherit
  when all 8.2 conditions are met; materially new families are excluded by
  default.
- Verified no-license allowlist enforcement remains unchanged: the existing
  `is_focusa_verified_no_license_family_allowed` classifier and its six constant
  arrays continue to gate verified-no-license access with the exact allowlist
  and blocked-family lists.
- Operation registry generator (scripts/generate-spec135-operation-contracts.py)
  continues to stamp `spec172_family` on all 108 operations; the 7
  recovery/read/diagnostic operations with null spec172_family are not
  value-producing mutations and remain correctly gated by their operation_class.
- No new tool becomes limited-access by default. Existing Operator families
  inherit safe implementation additions. Material/new/unknown boundaries
  fail closed.
