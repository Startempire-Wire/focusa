Bounded result
- Modeled the perpetual License Type entitlement (term `lifetime`) as a
  SEPARATE record from the bounded signed device lease. `LifetimeEntitlement`
  carries product, License Type, term, status, highest authority sequence,
  price version, family digest, node/seat limits; `DeviceCredentialWindow`
  carries only the bounded lease (node binding, lease sequence, refresh
  window, offline grace, authority key, status) and never carries — and never
  expands — product, price, License Type, family, feature, limit, node, or
  commercial right. Product↔License Type pairing is server-frozen
  (`focusa`↔`focusa_operator_lifetime_v1`, `uiai_engine`↔
  `uiai_operator_lifetime_v1`); caller-controlled grants fail closed.
- Lifecycle state machine (`LifetimeCredentialMachine::resolve`) decides from
  the two records: Active inside the signed refresh window; OfflineGrace
  inside the bounded 30-day grace; RecoveryOnly when the credential is
  expired or missing but the lifetime entitlement is live (lifetime right
  persists; verified recovery issues a replacement lease); DeniedRevoked when
  the lifetime entitlement is revoked (refund/revoke/higher sequence defeats
  every stale and offline credential); DeniedStale when the credential
  sequence is below the entitlement's highest authority sequence (replays and
  pre-rotation credentials never trusted); DeniedUnknown with no entitlement.
- Store/refresh surface: `PersistedLifetimeState` mirrors
  `PersistedAuthorityState` (schema, read, atomic write); `rotate_credential`
  advances the highest sequence and persists the rotated bounded lease without
  widening anything; `recover_credential` issues a replacement lease after
  expiry (refused when revoked); `apply_refund_or_revoke` marks the lifetime
  entitlement revoked at a strictly higher authority sequence;
  `rotate_key` re-signs under a new authority key; `resolve_with_snapshot`
  reconciles the persisted ledger with a freshly verified authority lease
  snapshot (verifier adapter).
- WPUIAI issuer (`spec172-lifetime-entitlement-ledger.v1.php`): one
  `wp_wpuiai_lifetime_entitlements` row per product, separate
  `wp_wpuiai_device_credentials` bounded leases; recordEntitlement accepts
  only the canonical Operator projection (License Type/term/sequence
  server-owned); refund/revoke at strictly higher sequence → revoked; refresh
  and recovery refused after revoke (`VERIFIED_LIMITED_ACCESS`); lost-device
  revoke is preservation-only; real RFC 8032 Ed25519 signatures (pure PHP,
  fixed synthetic seed) over canonical payload bytes.
- Cross-language vectors: one committed fixture is signed by the PHP issuer,
  independently signature-verified by Python cryptography (5/5) and by Rust
  ed25519-dalek (5/5), and the PHP harness regenerates it byte-identically
  (`fixture_byte_exact: true`). The joint machine agrees in all three
  languages on all 14 vectors (active/offline_grace/recovery_only/
  denied_stale/denied_revoked/denied_unknown).
