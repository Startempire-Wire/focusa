Bounded result
- Removed local/self-issued Evaluation from every installer and lifecycle
  surface. The lifecycle receipt class and entitlement state now spell
  `LimitedAccessReady` / `ActiveVerifiedLimited` (serde labels
  `limited_access_ready` / `active_verified_limited`) instead of
  `EvaluationReady` / `active_evaluation`; `AuthorizationSelection::Evaluation`
  and `LicensePosture::Evaluation` are replaced by `LimitedAccess` and
  `VerifiedLimitedAccess`. Product readiness still requires a
  signature-verified binding with the canonical policy digest, capability
  family, entitlement state, lease sequence, and recovery posture recorded on
  the receipt (Spec 152F simple-policy binding); unverified or forged
  product-ready claims fail closed (`UnverifiedProductReady`,
  `PolicyReconciliation`). Receipts and policy bindings never carry raw email,
  key, token, credential, customer, or card fields.
- `--eval` is intent forwarding only. In the Rust installer it maps to the
  shared activation client's `ActivationJourney::LimitedAccess`
  (verified-email limited activation); the plan and preflight report record
  `authority_limited_access`, `authority_existing_or_limited_access`, and
  `authority_limited_access_request` instead of the evaluation spellings.
  Noninteractive installs keep verified device-code authorization
  (`DeviceAuthorizationSession`/`AuthorityHttpClient`); interactive installs
  render the universal email → verify → offer → checkout/poll → key/lease
  flow through `run_activation_flow` and settle as `activated` or fail closed
  (`E_AUTHORITY_ACTIVATION_UNSETTLED`). No `persist_eval_license`, no
  `return Ok("eval")`, no `write_license_json`, and no `E_EVAL_ISSUED` path
  exists in the installer.
- Unix and PowerShell bootstrappers remain pure presenters: they reject raw
  credentials (`E_AUTHORITY_RAW_KEY_FORBIDDEN`), never create local
  evaluation/authority/receipt state, and converge on the single Rust handoff.
  Their help and dry-run output now disclose the Spec 172 verified-email
  limited-activation mapping for `--eval` while preserving the pinned 152E
  "authority-issued only" disclosure. Paid key flow remains EDD-backed: raw
  license keys cannot authorize installation on any channel.
- First-run presenters (install walkthrough + terminal-ui install sources)
  render status only; they contain no grant, policy, or evaluation
  vocabulary.
- New gate `tests/spec172_installer_limited_access_test.py` statically binds
  all channels (official/source/raw) to the same verified-limited or paid
  authority flow and proves self-issuance is impossible: 45 positive and 44
  negative checks, all surfaces, no unmasked email anywhere.
