  `amount_minor` equals `price_usd` in minor units.
- Every record configures lifetime duration (EDD SL license length 0),
  whole-order 30-day refund metadata (`whole_order_30_days`, `refund_days`
  30), exactly one operator seat, three shared nodes
  (`operator_shared_v1`, activation limit 3), and checkout disabled until
  validation passes (`checkout_enabled: false`, `sale_status:
  approved_not_yet_enabled`, EDD download status `draft`, block reason
  `awaiting_validation_pass`).
- The provisioning command is idempotent by construction: it derives the
  plan only from the server-owned contracts in canonical order, emits a
  stable SHA-256 idempotency key, fails closed on any invariant violation
  (record/offer mismatch, legacy or 453 reuse, checkout enablement, wrong
  price, caller-controlled fields), and prints a byte-identical redacted
  receipt on every run (verified by diffing two consecutive runs).
- Unrelated legacy WPUIAI downloads (16, 17, 21-25, 66, 452, 454-457) and
  Download 453 can never grant these License Types: the dedicated records
  never reference them, Download 453 remains quarantined with the explicit
  `implicit_focusa_mapping_forbidden` reason in the canonical registry, no
  protected offer maps 453, and the test's grant resolution fails closed
  for 453, legacy, credit-pack, unknown, and wrong-price downloads.
- No anonymous product capability, no local/self-issued grant, and no
  caller-controlled download, price, License Type, family, feature, limit,
  node, or commercial right. The receipt is explicitly redacted and carries
  no raw email, license key, token, credential, customer row, or card data.
- The canonical Spec 152E registry contract was intentionally left frozen
  (`assigned_edd_downloads: 0`, `mapping_status:
  approved_policy_blocked_edd_mapping`): flipping it to active/checkout is
  the separately approved validation/mapping work of later atoms
  (20.15.13/20.15.14 issuance). The dedicated Downloads contract binds real
  EDD Download/price records to the registry offers and keeps checkout
  disabled until that validation passes.
