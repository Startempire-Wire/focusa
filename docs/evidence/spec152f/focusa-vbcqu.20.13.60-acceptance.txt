    oversized verifiers and tampered stored hashes fail; magic-link verifier is bounded
    url-safe and binds registration + origin; challenge inputs with CRLF facade, non-UUID,

    additionally prove expired challenges (EMAIL_VERIFICATION_EXPIRED), max-attempt
    exhaustion, rate limiting without disclosure, and cross-facade/unknown-UUID error
    indistinguishability (enumeration resistance).

    EDD_ORDER_UNVERIFIED / ACCOUNT_MERGE_REVIEW_REQUIRED) until a verified-link review

    7 holds journaled, 0 licenses created).

    expired-CSRF, CSRF-replay, wrong/suffix origin, unknown product, absolute/unknown

    FACADE_CSRF_DENIED / FACADE_ORIGIN_DENIED / FACADE_PRODUCT_DENIED /
    FACADE_REDIRECT_DENIED / FACADE_REQUEST_DENIED); safe redirects stay bounded to
    registered callback handles.
  - arbitrary grants: agent envelope forbids email/key/lease/credential fields; the
    facade request contract forbids grants/features/limits/price/download/sender/redirect
    fields; the product registry offers are policy-blocked and unassigned; Spec 158
