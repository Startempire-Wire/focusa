Bounded result
- Added the facade policy presenter contract: branded facades and Focusa
  website presenters display the SAME canonical authority decision for every
  registered facade — capability family (base product or one of the four
  premium families: automation, team_remote, release_proof, premium_updates),
  the Evaluation/purchase/recovery action, and a safe masked status from
  authority output. They cannot select grants, prices, feature activation, or
  runtime policy, and they cannot turn dormant future-granularity or premium
  dimensions on or off.
- Rust contract (crates/focusa-license/src/facade_policy_presenter.rs):
  `FacadeFamily` (base/premium/always-reachable), `FacadeNextAction`
  (evaluate/purchase/recovery/manage), `FacadePolicyDecision::project`
  deriving only from the canonical `EntitlementStateDecision` +
  `CapabilityFamily` + authority status label, `facade_family`,
  `facade_next_action_for_posture`, `facade_next_action_for_status`, and
  `safe_masked_status` (frozen `FACADE_STATUS_ALLOWLIST`, masked pattern
  `^[^@]*\*[^@]*@[^@]+$`). Internal maintenance is not facade-presentable
  (fail closed). Frozen `FACADE_PRESENTER_FIELDS` (8 fields), 
  `FACADE_PRESENTER_FORBIDDEN_FIELDS` (16 fields), and
  `FACADE_ALWAYS_REACHABLE` (9 families, shared with menubar/TUI). The
  struct exposes no booleans, no setters, and no grant/price/feature/
  runtime-policy fields.
- Website presenter (public/activation/focusa-facade-policy-presenter.mjs):
  `projectFacadePolicyDecision(facadeId, envelope)` binds all six registered
  facades to the identical facade-independent projection (origin gate only);
  spoofed envelopes fail closed with FACADE_ORIGIN_DENIED /
  FACADE_POLICY_DENIED (unknown origin/family/posture, grants, prices,
  feature_activation, runtime_policy, dormant/premium toggles,
  product_code, limits, lease injection, raw email, redirect hijack,
  unknown extra fields). Unknown statuses never render (masked_status
  dropped); recovery_only always shows the recovery action. Frozen
  `facadePolicyContract` (schema focusa.spec152f.facade_policy_presenter.v1)
  mirrors the Rust field lists; no per-facade commercial override exists.
- Exact-origin/session/redirect rules remain Spec 152E-owned (facade
  registry, facade security, activation call stack untouched).
- No local/self-issued Evaluation, no caller-controlled product/price/
  grants, no presenter-owned commercial decision, no raw keys/tokens/
  customer PII in code, tests, or evidence; no 395 independent paywalls;
  customer data export, account control, repair, stable security update,
  rollback, and uninstall allowances untouched.
