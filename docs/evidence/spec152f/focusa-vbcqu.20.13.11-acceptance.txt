Bounded result
- Added FocusaSpec152eEmailDeliveryConsentHandler: records delivery outcomes
  (sent/delivered/bounced/complained) from email provider callbacks without raw
  tokens; manages bounce state (soft/hard) and suppression state (none/
  transactional/promotional/all); settles transactional consent independently
  from optional promotional consent; revokes promotional consent without
  affecting transactional consent; provides capability checks:
  canSendTransactional (never gated by promotional consent), canSendPromotional
  (requires promotional consent + no suppression), canVerifyIdentity (hard
  bounce and suppression cannot become verified identity).
- Hard bounces prevent all sending and verification.
- Soft bounces record but do not prevent transactional sending.
- Complaints (spam reports) suppress the reported message kind plus all
  promotional messaging.
- Promotional consent settlement is blocked for hard-bounced and suppressed
  identities.
- Transactional consent settlement is never blocked by bounce or suppression
  (promotional consent never gates transactional messages).
- All envelopes are masked: no raw email, tokens, secrets, or authority
  references in output.
- Added findByUuid, settleConsent, and revokePromotionalConsent to
  FocusaSpec152eEmailIdentityRepository (minimal additions to support the
  delivery/consent handler).
