Bounded result
- Added `LifecyclePolicyBinding` to every Spec 150A `LifecycleReceiptV1`:
  records the digest of the single embedded entitlement policy
  (`focusa_license::embedded_entitlement_policy_registry().digest()`), the
  canonical capability family (base_focusa for value-producing install/repair/
  rerun mutations, premium_updates for update, read_projection for inspect,
  account_recovery for uninstall/purge/rollback and every recovery or denied
  posture), the entitlement state, the lease sequence, the recovery posture,
  and the reconciled product-ready flag. The binding is derived only from the
  canonical registry and the receipt's own authority fields; no caller
  product/price/grant/family/policy identity is accepted, and receipts never
  record raw keys, tokens, credentials, or customer PII (structural by
  construction; verified in `receipt_policy_binding_never_records_raw_key_material`).
- Added `LifecycleReceiptV1::reconcile_policy()`: re-checks the recorded
  binding against the embedded registry digest and the family/state/sequence/
  recovery posture recomputed from the receipt's own fields; drifted or forged
  policy identity fails closed with the new `PolicyReconciliation` error.
  Structural validation (`validate_content`) enforces the policy-digest
  format, canonical family/state vocabulary, recovery-product exclusivity,
  lease-sequence equality with the flattened binding, and product-ready
  reconciliation; product-ready claims still require a verified signed lease
  (`UnverifiedProductReady` unchanged).
- Installer surfaces were already bound to the same simple policy and were
  not modified: the Rust installer acquires authority-backed
  Evaluation/paid leases through the device-code activation client, verifies
  envelopes before persistence, and performs entitlement acquisition before
  asset download (license gate precedes asset download); `install-focusa.sh`
  and `install-focusa.ps1` delegate to the canonical Rust installer, forward
  only intent (e.g. `--eval`), never create local Evaluation, and reject raw
  license keys/emails. Official and source installs therefore use the same
  policy with no duplicated commercial logic.
