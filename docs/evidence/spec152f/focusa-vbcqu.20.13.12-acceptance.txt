Bounded result
- Added FocusaSpec152eAccountPromotionService: one caller-owned idempotent
  transaction that, only for a mailbox-verified registration whose email digest
  binds the submitted email, (1) resolves or creates the EDD customer by exact
  verified email, (2) resolves or creates the authority account for that customer,
  (3) links the optional WordPress user and Stripe reference without creating
  duplicates, (4) resolves or creates the verified email identity bound to the
  resolved account and marks it primary (first) or linked, (5) persists
  transactional consent separately from optional promotional consent without
  overwriting earlier consent, (6) links prior evidence-backed EDD orders and
  licenses (order must be owned by the customer and status 'complete'; license
  must be owned by the customer and not revoked/disabled), and (7) advances the
  registration to account_promoted through the registration repository's new
  in-transaction primitive.
- Crash/retry is atomic: a failed attempt rolls back all writes (verified by row
  counts) and a retry yields exactly one account, one resolved customer, and one
  identity; identical replays return the stored bounded result with no duplicates.
- Conflicts enter review without partial writes:
  ACCOUNT_MERGE_REVIEW_REQUIRED for an identity already bound to another account,
  a WordPress user or Stripe customer already linked to another account, or an
  order/license already claimed by another account;
  EDD_ORDER_UNVERIFIED for orders not owned by the customer or non-complete
  (including refunded) orders; EDD_LICENSE_UNUSABLE for revoked/disabled or
  non-owned licenses.
- Unverified, mismatched, or expired input never promotes: unverified
  registration and wrong registration email both fail closed
  (EMAIL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED / ACCOUNT_EMAIL_MISMATCH); expired registrations
  fail with REGISTRATION_EXPIRED; changed requests cannot reuse an idempotency
  key (IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT); missing transactional consent is rejected.
- Email aliases are never merged through dot/plus rewriting: identity resolution
  uses the exact keyed digest only.
- The result envelope and idempotency payload are masked: no raw email, no
  secrets, no authority credentials. Promotion truth (purchase links, consent,
  account/customer/identity references) is journaled and rollback is
  preservation-only.
