Bounded result
- Cached base/premium grants resolve only within signed bounds: the signed
  lease is active inside the 90-day refresh window, degrades to offline_grace
  past it, and expires past the 30-day grace window (frozen 90+30 policy,
  lifetime term preserved). Premium features re-check the signed grace window
  at decision time (CachedGrantExpired / MissingCachedGrantExpiry); a premium
  feature that was not signed into the lease is never minted offline
  (MissingFeature). Base and premium decisions bind the authority lease
  sequence; no caller product/price/grant input is accepted.
- No node/grant/limit expansion: the declared node limit stays exactly three
  and the fourth node is denied even while a fresh paid or still-window-valid
  cached grant exists; caller-invented limit buckets and units above the
  authority-owned capacity are refused at reservation time; caller-controlled
  grants/price/node-limits are rejected on every commercial surface.
- Higher refund/revoke sequence wins: refund (account sequence 3) and revoke
  (account sequence 6) each settle the lease to a terminal recovery-only state
  with a signed refusal, overriding the still-window-valid cached offline
  grant; stale paid credentials can never refresh again; replay never bumps
  the sequence; terminal reactivation is denied; the verified account returns
  to limited mode with recovery allowances intact.
- Outage preserves recovery: during the outage the facade returns the
  canonical AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE recovery_only envelope (never terminal,
  retry preserved) and creates no customer, order, license, projection, node,
  or lease; the cached signed lease is the only execution authority inside its
  window; read/export/account-control/repair/rollback/stable-security-update/
  uninstall remain available while value-producing base mutations are denied.
- Direct core/API/CLI/worker/UIAI bypass attempts fail closed: wrong product,
  wrong node, stale sequence, revoked lease, caller-controlled grants/price/
  node-limits, child-token feature/limit widening (SCOPE_NOT_GRANTED), nonce
  replay, and worker dispatch without a base entitlement (or with a closed
  offline grace window) are all refused before side effects.
- Adversarial matrix recorded: schema
  focusa.spec152f.offline_adversarial_matrix.v1 with outage, stale_set, and
  bypass matrices all true; project data and recovery preserved end to end;
  rollback is preservation-only; no raw email/key/token/customer PII in any
  journal or evidence.
