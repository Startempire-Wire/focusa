Bounded result
- Added `BaseProductDecision` (entitled / limited / denied) and
  `resolve_base_focusa_product(product, state)`: one usable signed product
  entitlement for product `focusa` (Active paid lease or valid Offline Grace)
  gates the base product. `VerifiedNoLicense` resolves to the explicit manual
  one-project Focusa subset (limited); every other policy state denies
  value-producing core mutations. Wrong or prefixed products never satisfy the
  base gate. No caller-controlled product, price, grant, or feature input is
  accepted.
- Added `BASE_PRODUCT_CORE_COMPATIBILITY_IDS` and
  `base_product_compatibility_projection`: `focusa.core.mission`,
  `focusa.core.workpoint`, and `focusa.core.evidence` remain visible in leases,
  telemetry, and projections but resolve as parts of the base Focusa product —
  never separately purchased features. Stored feature values are
  non-authoritative projection claims; the base decision governs.
- Added `base_product_projection(snapshot)` in focusa-license: canonical,
  client-safe `BaseProductProjection` (schema `focusa.base_product_projection.v1`)
  derived only from the signed authority snapshot; fails closed with
  `EntitlementSnapshotMissing` when no snapshot exists. Authority
  Active/OfflineGrace map to usable base states; RecoveryOnly and Unactivated
  map to denied policy postures.
- Added `require_base_product()` in focusa-core with a
  `LicenseError::BaseProductRequired` error variant carrying the denied
  decision label for value-producing core mutation paths.
- Hardened the product boundary to accept only the exact authority-owned
  identifier `focusa`; casing, whitespace, aliases, and prefixed values fail
  closed rather than being normalized into a grant. The Spec 172
  `verified_no_license` posture remains limited to its explicit manual subset;
  it does not satisfy the full base-mutation gate.
