Bounded result
- Added FocusaSpec152eLegacyActivationAdapter: mailbox verification is
  mandatory before new-node activation, reissue, terminal delivery, or
  account merge for legacy EDD customers. The submitted EDD Software
  Licensing key must resolve to a usable license (not revoked/disabled)
  whose owning customer has the verified email as a primary or linked
  owner address, with a complete order, and the resolution must be
  evidence-backed (kind purchase_evidence | stripe_reconciliation |
  install_site_migration). Synthetic/unknown records and missing evidence
  remain quarantined (EDD_ORDER_UNVERIFIED). A key and an unrelated
  verified email fail closed with LICENSE_ACCOUNT_MISMATCH — raw matching
  email alone never transfers ownership. The adapter is read-only over EDD
  truth and returns a masked, deterministic envelope: no raw email, no raw
  key, no secrets; only masked license/customer/order/product references,
  allowed-purpose flags, and the pinned evidence digest.
- Extended FocusaSpec152eEddCustomerAdapter with bounded, exact
  email/license lookup primitives (never enumerating; key is never echoed
  in results, logs, or exceptions) and customerHasEmail() for exact
  owner-email proof against primary and verified linked addresses.
- Extended FocusaSpec152eAccountPromotionService with
  mergeLegacyVerified(): one idempotent, caller-owned transaction that
  re-resolves the legacy key inside the transaction, requires the same
  owner match and evidence-backed linkage, requires stronger evidence for
  conflicting paid records (ACCOUNT_MERGE_REVIEW_REQUIRED when the claimed
  purchase is foreign or points at a different order/license than the
  resolved key), journals the legacy evidence and its bounded digest on
  the purchase link, preserves existing order/license state (EDD rows are
  never modified; revoked/disabled licenses and non-complete orders are
  never reactivated; paid entitlement is never downgraded), and advances
  the registration to account_promoted. The shared promote core keeps
  promoteVerified() byte-for-byte behavior identical (verified-mode replay
  and digest unchanged).
- Failed merges write zero state (transaction rollback verified by row
  counts); identical replays return the stored bounded result with no
  duplicates; the result envelope and idempotency payload are masked.
